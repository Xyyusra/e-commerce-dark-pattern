Before you start: research study information

HCI research study by Hafsa Tanveer and Yusra Ahmed. Please read this page with the researcher.

⚠ DO NOT ENTER REAL CARD OR PERSONAL DETAILS

Pakien is a mock online store built for this research. It is not a real shop. Nothing is charged, nothing is delivered and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Never type your own card number, CVC, address, phone, email or password. Use only the made-up details given to you (also available from the Details to use button at the top of the store).

What you will do

Task 1: Shop aroundBrowse the store and choose one birthday gift for a friend. Add it to your cart.
Task 2: Check outGo to checkout and place the order using the made-up details.

Shop as you normally would and think aloud: say what you notice and why you make each choice. The tasks are shown in a banner at the top of the screen.

What we collect

  • A screen recording of this browser tab while you do the tasks. It is saved to the researcher's computer only, not uploaded.
  • Audio of you thinking aloud, recorded separately by the researcher.
  • Your participant ID (below), so we can match the recording with your questionnaire. We do not collect your name.
  • Recordings are seen only by the two researchers, used only for this course project, and deleted when it ends.

Your rights

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, by telling the researcher.
  • If you want your recording deleted later, give the researcher your participant ID.
